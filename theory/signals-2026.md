# Signals 2025–2026

*What changed in the world since the project began, and what it means for ORPHÉE.*
*Facts checked on 27 September 2026. Spotted an error? Open an issue: charter principle II applies to us too.*

The 2026 revision of the ORPHÉE narrative rests on one observation. Two movements are crossing:

1. **The readers of the past have never been so powerful.** Carbonised scrolls are read without being opened. Ice cores bring back air that is 1.2 million years old. Telescopes map the light echoes of explosions seen centuries ago.
2. **Manufacturing a past that never existed has never been so easy.** Memorials are asking platforms to remove AI-generated "archive photos", and the EU now requires synthetic content to be labelled.

ORPHÉE stands on that border. The simulator makes images and says so. The research is interested only in real traces.
